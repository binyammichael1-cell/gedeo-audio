"Gedeo green land" refers to the Gedeo Zone in Ethiopia, a landscape dominated by a unique and ancient agroforestry system where the Gedeo people cultivate coffee and enset (a banana-like plant) alongside diverse tree species. This system is known for its high biodiversity and sustainability, which is further protected by the Gedeo people's strong cultural traditions that emphasize environmental protection, including strict rules against deforestation and a spiritual connection to the forest.

Crops: The system's main components are enset and coffee, which are crucial for the food security of the community.
Biodiversity: Each 100m² plot can host a high diversity of trees, with up to 50 species from 35 families. Sustainability: This method of land use has sustained the population for generations and is recognized as a sustainable land management system. Baalle is a traditional, indigenous democratic system of governance for the Gedeo people in Ethiopia, regulating political, social, economic, and religious life.
It is a rotational system where leadership changes every eight years, and it is based on principles of accountability, consensus-building, and conflict resolution. The system is organized into age-based classes that progress through a series of grades.

There are 45 Laws

1. Gede’iixe aada’n ci’atika quuphphe shiyya cubboten. Iitta kaddoole ilendaaxxi anno rertaan/baxxaab hiyyende addatendammaxxeeten.

2. Hirreessiinxa haqqe mini’a adhdhe e’yya haranga hoyyendeexxen. Hirreessiinxa giira giidhdhinoole finni gosa muddaan hine sodaantammaxxeete.

3. Hashsha qe’ya woxedha,umo haaxxa,seemma gophpha, mine fiya,gaffe hoyyendeexxen buutotinna hiyyumma ye’atishshaten hine addantammaxxeeten.

4. Manji mitti nage’iinke mine’n seemma haguulqe e’yya haranga hoyyendeexxen.E’yyeechchi udumanna haguuggo tilqa giddeten kada gophphoole farotishsha uudendaan.

5. Miniinxi ama miniinke annikanna amaka summa bira he’nee’naanna kadi he’nnebaak ye’a hoyyendeexxen.

6. Meyyaxxi belto seerik miniinxe ama kadatee hachcho hiyyemaake Weeshiinke wodi’inni aanshedha hasissaan.

7. Miniinxi ama haleenshita’n miniinke annike anninni afendoole hadi’a wirri hitee aaggaa malee saxxe metabaan. Magenokenaa’n ergemeeke araarishsha laakko’endaan.

8. Miniinke anniki anni galchaaxxe songonaa’n miniixi ama qaxxaamurtaa wodda miniinke annik anni bira hedhenna hedhanna gophee “anke anna balleesseenne seera ani’a uwwi” hitebaak qaxxaamurtabaan.

9. Manjiin belti biniinchik welti maaliinxa afensha assabbaan, asseexxe kaddoole farotishsha laakko’endaan.

10. Anno ilendeechchi uduma ha’nura ibbeebaak shuta haandebaakinna summa fulchinebaak baxxoole wo’mmi manjishsha laakko’a gonphammaxxee hirreessake’n moogendabaan. “Qarra” hinaaxxe bakka’n imarree ugginaa malee.

11. Meyyaxxi belto aanshi mini giddike miya’n dagoole farratishsha laakko’endammaxxe miyi iki hunemaan.

12. Ayyinna kakkichcho gosha hoyyendeexxen. Isoo gosheeke manjixxi finninna gosa goshendaan, illetinna baxxaan hine addantammaxxeeten.

13. Dayyo dayyo badindoole elochchobaan weeshshiinka muuxxichcho goshshe badindoole meyyaxxa male ilendabaan hine addantaan.

14. Miniinxe amaxxi ama lubbendoole beltotik miniink anni bale giddonna uggaba buttinanna buyyabaan.

15. Lame miniinxa ama afeeki miniinki anni mitte miniinxi ama lubbendoole tazgaari fule ke’ebaak lubboti hexxeexxe miniinxe amanni maaliinxa afensha assabaan.

16. Mitti manji labbaka belto I’lebaak lubbemoole fichchi isik woddeessiinke fichcha hiyyemati ye’emaan.Fichcha okkonenaa’ninna haqichchokanna adhdhinabaan. Buutotinna wogga haruunsaan hine hendammaxxeeten.

17. Manji adamo fulee’na adamoke’n biniichik welti lubbemoole miyi isiki adhdhemabaan okkoonni hunemaan.

18. Miniink anni miniinxa ama lolaa’na mete mososso,gaadi’a e’yyitoole, ma’ni’a baqattoole lola waala giddeten. Buutotinna mososso,ma’nanna gaadi lumoka ulfinna afeechcho kadeexxeeten.

19. Wo’di’i fulee’na olla’I reebe xorseechchi uduma haqqe gubateeke minxxa adhdhaaki hedhebaan. Buutotinna gubateeke minixxi haqe mine e’yitoole giiratixxa bala geessissabaashshaten.

20. Mini gubatee’na wodi’I fulee male gubatee hinabaan. Buutotinna giirati halo abida gonphammaxxeeten.

21. Meyyaxxi belto ciniintoti lubbendoole ciniintoti rertee hinabaan, duuloke’n lubbendeen hinaa malee.

22. Anno ilendeechchi uduma ha’nura ibbebaak summi fulebaak shuta haandabaan. Buutotinna wo’mma manjo hine heda gonphammaxxeeten.

23. Ayyi manjinna bofa shiyeechchi uduma shiye butta uggabaan harichoke’n qarabaa malee. Buutotinna bofi muuxate ke’eexxe kaddoole shimmaki annichchi reyaan hine addantammaxxeeten.

24. Gede’iine aada’n badhaasakeenna kaddi muratee moja uunaarabaan. Buutotinna moji xe’yyake kademmaxxe xe’ya wodo idaggaan hine addantammaxxeeten.

25. Gede’iinke seera’n gaaffeti lomuki shiixxooka miicoolenna gattowwaal hiyyi malee ati balleessiteetten hinabaan.

26. Gadichchiink belti yookin miniinxi ama lubbendoole gatexxe hulanaa’n malee edii’n reenshi fulabaan. Gadichchikinna oodhabaan buutotinna gadichchi ardiinke malee isixxe maateke calla kada gophemmaxxeeten, oodhoole manji mudaan hine addantammaxxeeten.

27. Mitti manji ibeechcho buttii’n afeexxe kaddoole adhdhe mini’a aagabaan, sase weldeenna’n ugginaa malee.

28. Gede’iinxe aada’n manji mitti ileme kalaale geyeechchi uduma gosa ifixxa aadhaarabaan. Buutotinna wogga insa’nexxi haru’mitaan ittan finni insa’nekinna nophphabaan hine hendammaxxeeten.

29. Angafi labbaki belti anniinke ofo’lichcho’n ofo’laarabaan buutotinna angafi woraashete kademmaxxe anni lubboti hedhee’naa worassa farraten kaddemmaxxeeten.

30. Gede’iinxe aada’n angafi hedhee’na udumik riqichcha foofabaan. Buuto lumollakee uunaannoke ulfinnan.

31. Maate giddo anni he’nee’na belti edise anga aanshedhabaan,anni aanshedheeke wode’e’ninna hana aanshedhabaan. Buutotinna belti anniinxe shi’na hine addantammaxxeeten.

32. Gede’iinxe aada’n moori haranga gibemeechchon, kaddaa’naanni mitti raakkichchi manji waasa mooreexxi leedde bire uggoole, fira isike kadeexxa annichchi ege’naan hoolanna assisabaan.

33. Oodhishshati mine’n ita angii’n ibbee’na adhdhinaarabaan buutotinna rewo aadhe aagaan hine sodaantammaxxeeten.

34. Anninna ama indaa’na ita gudeeda insa’neke’n yookin ma’naten ibboole adhdhine indabaan oosetee uunaan buutotinna oose gudeediinxe gumman hine hendammaxxeeten.

35. Anniinxe’n yookin akka’iinxe oone’n mine ijaadhdha hoyyendeexxen. Buutotinna wogga haru’mitaaan hine addantammaxxeeten.

36. Mine ijaarraa wodda buttina fannaa wodda mososso ontammaxxe bakka’n weeshsho kaansaan, kada gophphoole eloxxi wodo geltabaan hine addantammaxxeeten.

37. Mitti manji aadhaa wodda taakkaka weeshshiinka hochcho fayaaki manji “jaalo” hiyyeme ye’emaake kadaa wodda. Jaaloyyon mitte ma’naninna galloole maaliinxa afensha assa haranga cubbotenna farraten hine addantaan.

38. Wodi’I adhdheeka manjo galaaniin qarqara moongaa male mini’a adhdhine aangabaan. Buutotinna wode’eke’n halo abixxa gonphammaxxeeten.

39. Mini ijaarraa wodda miniinxe mososso’a bita’i gama’n hexxeexxa kuta meyyaxxee unaan. Buutotinna meyyaxxi labbakixxe bita’i gama hiyyendammaxxeeten.

40. Aadheeki belti anniinxe ma’na’n diiphabaan.

41. Heerundeexxi belto amati wolti diiphphabaan anniinxe ma’nna’ninna diiphphabaan, beltikinna beltotik anninni hoone jeejinninna diiphaachchobbaan.

42. Gede’iinxe aada’n labbaka malee meyyaxxi jila ofoldaabaan, buutotinna ciniintotixxe yanna’n rakkattaan hine hendammaxxeten.

43. Odhishshatixxe yanna’n maate saami hite ooxxabaa, anno baxxoole”bushoottee”, dargageessi reyoole”lubbemee” lumo manji reyoole “hancufa liqiinse” hinaan, lumolli he’nee’na oosichchi oodhishsha safabaan, safoole gara affebaaxxi oodhishsha rewote yexxaan hine addantammaxxeeten.

The Dorse houses are completely woven houses from the leaves of false bananas over a structure of bent branches. According to the villagers, the bottom parts of the huts are eaten by termites and over the decades, they slowly lose height and width; eventually they are used as utility shags.

Traditional Ethiopian houses, often called tukuls, are built using local materials like wood, mud, and thatch. Construction typically involves building a circular or rectangular frame, then filling the walls with mud mixed with straw and dung, and topping it with a conical, thatched roof. The process can vary depending on the region, with some homes also using stones for foundations and walls.

About “Darraro”

It is a Celebration of Growth and Prosperity

The Gedeo people's New Year is called Dararo. The name means "to cross over" and symbolizes the transition from hardship to abundance, marking a new agricultural cycle after the coffee harvest. Celebrations include cultural events, dances, feasts, and gatherings for thanksgiving and to pray for the new season.
Symbolism: Dararo, which can also mean "flower," signifies the start of a new cycle and the beauty of the season.
Timing: It is celebrated after the coffee harvest, typically in January or February.

Activities:
- Gathering at sacred sites like Michille Grissa for rituals and blessings.
- Feasting, dancing, and music.
- Organizing symposiums on Gedeo culture and language.
- Praying to the supreme god, Megeno, for protection and favor.
- Preparing for the upcoming farming season.
Location: The celebrations often conclude in the administrative capital, Dilla, but are celebrated across the Gedeo Zone.
